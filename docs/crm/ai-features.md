# AI features on a lead

Grouped under **More** on the lead drawer, and again on the **More** tab as **AI assistant**. They are not peers of Convert to CRM.

| Action | What it does | When to use it |
|---|---|---|
| **AI summary** | Writes a short summary of this lead into the insight panel | Before a call, when you need the history in one place |
| **Next best action** | Suggests the next sales step (the existing NBA insight) | When the lead is stale and you are unsure whether to call, qualify, or convert |
| **Rescore** | Recalculates score and Cold / Warm / Hot. Does not require the AI module | After new calls, notes, or qualification answers |
| **Find duplicates** | Lists other leads that match this one, with **Merge into this** | Before creating a second lead for the same phone |

AI summary and next best action stay disabled when the tenant does not have the AI module. The button title says why (`upgradeHint`). Rescore and Find duplicates stay available.

The insight body is shown under the buttons on the More tab. It is the same insight object the AI module already returns.
