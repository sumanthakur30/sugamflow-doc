## Executive summary

**SugamFlow** is a cloud-based operations platform for Indian SMBs. **Polyclinic / Clinic** (POLYCLINIC) OPD-focused clinic profile: reception queue, doctor dashboard, consultation pad, e-prescription, lab referrals, and pharmacy handoff within one healthcare tenant.

---

## Who is this for?

| Fit | Examples |
|-----|----------|
| **Multi-doctor polyclinics** | Several specialists, one front desk |
| **Single-doctor clinics** | GP, paediatric, dental (basic) |
| **Diagnostic + consultation** | Clinic that refers to own path lab |
| **Trust healthcare networks** | Clinic + lab + pharmacy siblings |

**Typical users:** Owner, counter staff, doctor/reception (healthcare).

4. Indian GST posture for clinic billing where applicable
