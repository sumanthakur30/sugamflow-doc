## Executive summary

**SugamFlow** is a cloud-based operations platform for Indian SMBs. **Pharmacy** (PHARMACY) Full pharmacy profile with medicine master, strip/tablet units, prescription dispense from polyclinic, tenant-wide catalog, and pharmacy-chain procurement profile.

## Industry software landscape (India)

| Category | Representative products | Typical strength | Typical limitation |
|----------|-------------------------|------------------|-------------------|
| **Pharmacy ERP** | Marg Pharma, Medeil, Pharmasoft | Schedule H, batch, expiry | Desktop / training |

| **Mobile billing** | Vyapar (pharmacy mode) | Quick start | Rx workflow limited |
| **Hospital pharmacy** | HMIS modules (Birlamedisoft, etc.) | IPD integration | Heavy & costly |
