## Executive summary

**SugamFlow** is a cloud-based operations platform for Indian SMBs. **Restaurant / F&B** (RESTAURANT) Restaurant profile with guest-centric labels, F&B-oriented GST flows, combo/add-on billing playbooks, and inventory for ingredients and packaged items.

| Fit | Examples |
|-----|----------|
| **Restaurants & diners** | Table service, takeaway |
| **Cafés & bakeries** | Quick service counters |
| **Fast food** | High-volume counter |

**Typical users:** Owner, counter staff.
